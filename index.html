Expiration notifications

Email reminders A weekly list every Sunday at 7 AM of anything expiring in the next 7 days, plus a final reminder the morning before each item expires. They're sent by a small script in your own Google account that reads your synced Drive data, so they arrive even when this app is closed. Items you mark as used are skipped.
